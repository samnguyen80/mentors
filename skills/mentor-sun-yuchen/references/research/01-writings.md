## I. Formal Publications

### 1.1 《这世界既残酷也温柔》 (This World Is Both Cruel and Gentle) (2017)

- **Type**: Autobiography / inspirational
- **Publisher**: Jiuzhou Press (九州出版社), March 2017
- **Pages**: 288 pages, list price RMB 39.80
- **ISBN**: 9787510850660
- **Preface author**: Feng Lun (冯仑), founder of Vantone Group (万通集团)
- **Core narrative**: "In ten years, from a third-tier university admission score to Peking University, from studying in the US to starting a business back in China, from having nothing to financial freedom"
- **Chapter themes**: Choosing your own path of dreams, not looking for a secure job, independence, investing and entrepreneurship, financial freedom
- **Key quotes**:
  - "This era is more competitive, its growth wilder, its rewards richer, and its risks more startling" (「这个时代竞争更为激烈、生长更为野蛮、收获更为丰厚、风险更为惊人」)
  - "What matters most in life is the scale of one's vision (格局). Vision is like the gene of a species: it determines growth from the very source" (「人生最重要的是格局，格局就如同一个物种的基因，从最源头决定了物种的生长」)
- **Narrative packaging analysis**: This book set the tone for Justin Sun's "grassroots comeback" persona: a third-tier-score candidate who made it to Peking University, studied at the University of Pennsylvania, and achieved financial freedom, a complete story of class mobility
- **Sources**: [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/这世界既残酷也温柔-孙宇晨/dp/B01LTICE1E) / [Douban](https://book.douban.com/subject/26984629/) / [Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/147050303)
- **Credibility**: First-hand (his own book)

## IV. Technical Whitepapers

### 4.1 TRON Whitepaper (2017)

- **Release date**: 2017
- **Core claim**: Build decentralized internet infrastructure
- **Plagiarism controversy (erupted January 2018)**:
  - **Accuser**: Protocol Labs founder Juan Benet pointed out on Twitter that at least 9 pages of TRON's English whitepaper were copied from the IPFS or Filecoin papers
  - **Third-party verification**: BuzzFeed News commissioned Quetext for a plagiarism analysis; the similarity score exceeded 15%, with entire passages, diagrams and phrases identical
  - **Vitalik Buterin's jab**: The Ethereum co-founder mocked the TRON team for raising the efficiency of whitepaper writing with "Ctrl+C + Ctrl+V"
  - **Justin Sun's response**: He claimed the Chinese version of the whitepaper had detailed citations and that important details were omitted when volunteers translated the English version
- **Core contradiction**: It claimed technical innovation, yet its foundational technical document was confirmed to have been heavily plagiarized, and the response dodged the real issue
- **Sources**: [BuzzFeed News](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/ryanmac/cryptocurrency-white-paper-plagiarism-tron-justin-sun) / [CoinCentral](https://coincentral.com/community-accuses-tron-plagiarizing-whitepaper/) / [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/justin-suns-controversies-plagiarism-teslas-warren-buffett-kidney-stones-and-a-deleted-apology/) / [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/hardfork/2018/01/15/cryptocurrency-startup-tron-disaster/)
- **Credibility**: First-hand (the whitepaper itself) + first-hand (independent third-party verification)

## XI. How the Educational Background Is Packaged

### Key Education Milestones

| Time | Credential | Actual situation | Packaging |
|------|------------|------------------|-----------|
| ~2011 | Peking University (Bachelor of History) | Obtained bonus points through the New Concept Essay Competition, entering Peking University from a third-tier-score level | "From third-tier score to Peking University" became the core comeback narrative |

| ~2013-2014 | University of Pennsylvania (Master's in East Asian Studies) | Studied in a liberal arts department, not the Wharton School | Often vaguely described as "UPenn," evoking Wharton; audited courses at Wharton during that period |
| 2014 | Davos Global Shaper | A World Economic Forum program | Stacked with the "Jack Ma protege" label to reinforce a global-elite image |
| 2015-2018 | Hupan University | An entrepreneur training program founded by Jack Ma (not a traditional degree) | "Jack Ma's protege" and "first post-90s graduate" used repeatedly |

### Analysis of Packaging Patterns

1. **Blurring credentials**: The two characters for "UPenn" (宾大) carry a Wharton halo, while the actual degree is a Master's in East Asian Studies
2. **Standardized comeback narrative**: "Third-tier score → Peking University → UPenn → financial freedom" became a fixed sentence pattern
3. **Stacking famous associations**: Peking University + UPenn + Davos + Hupan University + Jack Ma + Buffett + Trump, each step linking to a bigger name
4. **Label preemption**: The "first post-90s XX" pattern is used repeatedly: first post-90s Hupan graduate, youngest Chinese commercial astronaut, youngest on the Asia edition of Forbes 30 Under 30, etc.

## XII. Core Contradictions

### 12.1 Decentralization vs. Centralized Control

- **Claim**: Building decentralized internet infrastructure
- **Reality**: Personally controls most of the TRON tokens; nominally an "advisor" to HTX, yet multiple legal documents describe him as exercising "effective control" and "common ownership" over entities such as HTX and Poloniex
- **External assessment**: "a 'decentralised' network whose economics and governance are in practice dictated by one man and his orbit of entities": "he is both decentralisation advocate and central planner"
- **Source**: [Medium/Global Times Singapore](https://medium.com/@GlobalTimesSingapore/justin-sun-wants-to-control-the-future-of-crypto-the-question-is-how-much-he-already-does-5ca818c38536)
- **Credibility**: Second-hand (investigative reporting + legal documents)

### 12.2 Technical Innovation vs. Whitepaper Plagiarism

- **Claim**: TRON is the world's leading blockchain operating system
- **Reality**: The whitepaper was confirmed by a third-party tool to have heavily plagiarized IPFS and Filecoin (9+ pages, similarity >15%)
- **Response strategy**: Blamed the translation volunteers; never directly addressed the plagiarized content itself
- **Source**: [BuzzFeed News](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/ryanmac/cryptocurrency-white-paper-plagiarism-tron-justin-sun)
- **Credibility**: First-hand (independent third-party verification)

### 12.3 Advocating Transparency vs. Opaque Reserves

- **Claim**: Publicly criticized Coinbase's cbBTC for lacking transparency
- **Reality**: His own Poloniex could not provide proof of reserves (the whereabouts of USD 1.3 billion of BTC unknown); USDD quietly removed USD 732 million of bitcoin collateral
- **Sources**: [Protos](https://protos.com/poloniex-and-the-1-3b-bitcoin-question/) / [DL News](https://www.dlnews.com/articles/defi/justin-sun-responds-after-usdd-stablecoin-removes-bitcoin/)
- **Credibility**: First-hand (on-chain data) + second-hand (investigative reporting)

### 12.4 Anti-Traditional Finance vs. Embracing Political Power

- **Claim**: Decentralization, trustlessness, code is law
- **Reality**: Invested USD 75 million in the Trump family's crypto project → the SEC paused its case against him after Trump took office → settled for USD 10 million
- **Later reversal**: In September 2025, World Liberty Financial froze more than USD 100 million of Justin Sun's WLFI tokens, and Justin Sun publicly "pleaded" for them to be unfrozen
- **Sources**: [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2025/02/28/business/crypto-mogul-trump-coins-civil-fraud-charges) / [Fortune](https://fortune.com/crypto/2025/09/05/justin-sun-donald-trump-world-liberty-financial-blacklisted-crypto-address-wlfi/) / [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/09/05/trump-justin-sun-world-liberty-financial)
- **Credibility**: First-hand (SEC filings + on-chain data + public reporting)

### 12.5 SEC Allegations in Detail (March 2023 → Settlement in March 2026)

- **Plaintiff**: U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)
- **Defendants**: Justin Sun + Tron Foundation Limited + BitTorrent Foundation Ltd. + Rainberry Inc.
- **Core allegations**:
  1. Unregistered offering of securities (TRX and BTT)
  2. Obtained USD 31 million in illegal proceeds through secondary-market sales of TRX
  3. Between April 2018 and February 2019, directed employees to carry out more than 600,000 wash trades of TRX, 4.5 million to 7.4 million TRX per day
  4. Paid celebrities for promotion while requiring that the compensation not be disclosed (8 celebrities involved, including Lindsay Lohan, Jake Paul, Soulja Boy, Lil Yachty, Ne-Yo and Akon)
- **Outcome**: In March 2026, the SEC ended the lawsuit under the Trump administration, and Justin Sun's affiliated companies paid USD 10 million to settle
- **Sources**: [SEC.gov](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2023-59) / [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-05/sec-ends-case-against-crypto-billionaire-justin-sun) / [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2023/03/22/sec-hits-jake-paul-lindsey-lohan-justin-sun-with-crypto-violations.html)
- **Credibility**: First-hand (official SEC documents)

## XVII. Research Conclusion

Justin Sun's public writings and systematic content present a highly self-consistent narrative system, which can be summarized as five progressive layers:

1. **Grassroots comeback figure** (2017 autobiography) → 2. **Technology evangelist** (whitepaper, monograph) → 3. **Financial-freedom mentor** (Ximalaya, speeches) → 4. **Global elite collector** (art collection worth hundreds of millions) → 5. **Political player** (diplomat, guest of honor of the president)

But this narrative system contains several structural contradictions: the decentralization ideal vs. centralized control, technical innovation vs. whitepaper plagiarism, advocating transparency vs. opaque reserves, anti-traditional finance vs. embracing political power.

His core competence lies not in technical innovation but in **manipulation of the attention economy**. As "Sun-ology" sums it up: controversy itself is value, and consensus and attention decide everything. The psychological foundation of his methodology is that he can sustain the capacity to act and keep his narrative coherent under extreme pressure (the SEC lawsuit, global mockery), which analysts consider "virtually impossible to transplant."
