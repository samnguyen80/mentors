## II. Timeline of Major Decisions (Chronological)

### Decision 1: Founding TRON + ICO (August-September 2017)

**Background**: The 2017 ICO boom, before the Chinese government had banned ICOs. From his experience at Ripple and Peiwo, Justin Sun saw room for a decentralized-internet narrative.

**Actions**:
- Released the TRON whitepaper in August 2017 and completed the ICO in September, raising about $70 million
- The ICO finished just before the Chinese government's ban (September 4, 2017)
- In January 2018 the TRX token briefly rose to about $0.30, with a market cap in the top ten

**Whitepaper plagiarism controversy (January 2018)**:
- Juan Benet, founder of IPFS/Filecoin, publicly pointed out on Twitter that at least 9 pages of the English TRON whitepaper were highly similar to the Filecoin and IPFS whitepapers
- A plagiarism-detection firm gave a similarity score above 15%, with whole passages and diagrams copied directly
- Community comparisons also found technical descriptions copied from Ethereum and BitTorrent
- Justin Sun responded that the original Chinese version had full citations, and that the English version omitted them when volunteers translated it
- TRON then took the original whitepaper off its official website and uploaded a revised version
- Market cap fell rapidly from its high of $12 billion

**Actual result**: Despite the reputational damage, the TRON project survived and kept developing; as of 2026, TRX is still a mainstream coin in the top 20 by market cap.

**Consistency between words and actions**: ❌ **Inconsistent**. The "translation problem" explanation is hard to reconcile: identical diagrams and technical descriptions across whole passages cannot be explained by translation omissions. But Justin Sun successfully diverted attention, and the project did not end because of it.

### Decision 2: Acquiring BitTorrent (June 2018)

**Background**: TRON faced "vaporware coin" skepticism and needed a real product and user base to add credibility. BitTorrent is the world's largest P2P file-sharing protocol, with over 100 million monthly active users.

**Actions**:
- Began talks with BitTorrent in September 2017 and signed a letter of intent in January 2018
- Completed the acquisition for about $140 million in cash (purchased in his personal name)
- Subsequently issued the BTT token on top of BitTorrent (TRC10 standard), conducting an IEO on Binance Launchpad that sold out within minutes
- Launched BitTorrent File System (BTFS) and BitTorrent Speed

**Stated rationale**: Build a decentralized internet by combining BitTorrent's P2P architecture with the TRON blockchain.

**Actual result**:
- Brought a real user base and technical infrastructure to the TRON ecosystem
- The BTT token gave Justin Sun a new fundraising/liquidity tool
- BitTorrent's actual product innovation was limited, technical integration was limited, and it served more as a narrative and a token-issuance platform

**Consistency between words and actions**: ⚠️ **Partially consistent**. The acquisition itself was a substantive action, but the vision of a "decentralized internet" was realized only to a limited degree. BitTorrent became more a token-issuance platform than a vehicle for technological revolution.

### Decision 5: The Steem Community Hostile Takeover Incident (February-March 2020)

**Background**: Steem is a decentralized content blockchain. Steemit Inc. held a large pre-mined stake of STEEM tokens (the "ninja-mined stake"), and the community had previously reached an understanding with Steemit that these tokens would not be used for governance voting.

**Timeline of actions**:
1. **February 14, 2020**: Through the TRON Foundation, acquired Steemit Inc. from founder Ned Scott for about $8 million, obtaining about 70 million STEEM tokens (about 20% of total supply)
2. **Community counterattack**: Steem's witnesses froze the voting rights of Justin Sun's STEEM holdings through a soft fork (Soft Fork 22.2) to protect community governance
3. **Hostile takeover**: Justin Sun joined forces with the three exchanges Binance, Huobi, and Poloniex to **vote using users' STEEM tokens held in exchange custody**, replacing all 20 top elected representatives (witnesses) of Steem
4. **Community pushback**: The community reorganized the vote and regained control of 11 of the 20 witness seats
5. **Binance and Huobi apologized afterwards** and withdrew their votes
6. **Final split (March 20, 2020)**: The Steem community hard-forked to create the Hive blockchain, excluding Steemit Inc.'s tokens and breaking completely free of Justin Sun's control

**Core controversy**: Exchanges using customer-custodied assets to vote in governance was a serious abuse of the PoS governance mechanism, and one of the most controversial events in crypto industry history.

**Actual result**:
- Justin Sun got the Steemit platform but lost the core community and developers
- Hive did well after the fork, and Steem gradually declined
- The incident became a classic case in blockchain governance history (as a cautionary tale)
- It exposed the systemic risk of exchanges holding excessive power in PoS governance

**Consistency between words and actions**: ❌ **Severely inconsistent**. Justin Sun claims to support decentralization, yet used the power of centralized exchanges to forcibly seize control of a decentralized community. This is the case with the largest gap between words and actions among all his decisions.

**Sources**: [CoinDesk](https://www.coindesk.com/tech/2020/02/24/justin-sun-bought-steemit-steem-moved-to-limit-his-power), [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/38050/steem-steemit-tron-justin-sun-cryptocurrency-war), [CryptoBriefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/tron-executes-hostile-takeover-steem-exchanges-collude/), [CoinTelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/justin-sun-accused-of-bribing-his-way-to-the-top-of-the-steem-hierarchy) ⭐⭐⭐ (verifiable on-chain)

The product did launch and operate, but moving from a "decentralized algorithmic stablecoin" to "reserve-backed" amounts to negating the original technical route. The timing of launching a similar product during the Terra collapse exposed an opportunistic tendency.

### Decision 10: The "Rescue" Operation During the FTX Collapse (November 2022)

**Background**: FTX collapsed in November 2022, and Binance announced it was abandoning its acquisition.

**Actions**:
- Justin Sun tweeted that he was "putting together a solution"
- Promised he could commit "billions of dollars" to rescue FTX
- Said debt investment, equity investment, and a full acquisition were all under consideration
- Set up a 1:1 exchange credit line for TRON-ecosystem tokens on FTX (TRX, BTT, JST, SUN, HT), allowing users to move TRON-ecosystem tokens off FTX to external wallets

**Actual result**:
- The 1:1 exchange channel was indeed opened and helped FTX users holding TRON-ecosystem tokens (this was a real action)
- The "billions in rescue funds" never materialized
- TRX at one point surged 4000% on FTX (an abnormal swing amid drained liquidity)
- Overall, it was a successful PR operation rather than a substantive full rescue

**Consistency between words and actions**: ❌ **Partially inconsistent**. The promise of "billions in rescue funds" was not honored, but the 1:1 exchange for TRON-ecosystem token holders was a real action and showed some crisis-response capability. The pattern of this operation: first seize the headlines with a grand promise, then deliver part of the value through smaller but real actions.

### Decision 11: The SEC Lawsuit and Response Strategy (March 2023 - March 2026)

**SEC allegations (March 22, 2023)**:
- Sold unregistered securities (TRX and BTT) through the TRON Foundation, the BitTorrent Foundation, and Rainberry Inc.
- Large-scale wash trading to manipulate the TRX market: directing employees to execute **hundreds of thousands of wash trades** between accounts he controlled
- Paid celebrities (8 of them, including Akon, Lindsay Lohan, and Jake Paul) to promote TRX and BTT, **without disclosing the compensation**
- The 8 celebrities agreed to pay settlements totaling more than $400,000

**Justin Sun's response strategy**:
1. **Diplomatic immunity**: Claimed immunity based on his Grenadian diplomat status (rejected by the court in 2024)
2. **Active legal defense**
3. **Investing in political relationships**: Invested heavily in Trump-related crypto projects (WLFI, $TRUMP), totaling over $213 million
4. **Physical avoidance**: Has so far never appeared in a US courtroom and has consistently stayed outside the US
5. **Waiting for the political winds to change**

**Final outcome (March 5, 2026)**:
- After the Trump administration took office, the SEC withdrew **all charges** against Justin Sun personally and against the TRON Foundation and BitTorrent Foundation
- Rainberry Inc. (formerly BitTorrent Inc.) paid a $10 million settlement
- Justin Sun and the related companies "neither admit nor deny" any wrongdoing
- Democratic lawmakers including Elizabeth Warren sharply criticized it, saying the SEC had become "Trump's lap dog"
- House Democrats wrote to the SEC chair, pointing out that this was "pay-to-play" (paying to escape liability)

**Consistency between words and actions**: ⚠️ **Strategy succeeded, but the means were highly controversial**. The resolution of the SEC case closely matches the timeline of Justin Sun's large investments in Trump's crypto projects. House Democrats explicitly pointed to a causal link. Whether or not there is direct causation, that perception cuts both ways. The abrupt departure of the SEC enforcement chief shortly after the case settled further deepened the perception of political interference.

## IV. Summary of Decision Patterns

### Pattern 1: "Deny first, admit later"
Recurs in the Poloniex acquisition (first denied involvement, later admitted), the HTX acquisition (denied being the actual buyer), and the Grenadian ambassador term (first said he was still in office, later admitted it had ended). Justin Sun tends to deny involvement/control first, and admit it only when the evidence is conclusive or when it benefits him.

### Pattern 2: The attention economy as the core driver
From the Buffett lunch ($4.56 million), the sky-high banana ($6.24 million), the Blue Origin seat ($28 million), and the $TRUMP dinner (~$20 million), to the rescue statement during the FTX collapse, he is essentially an attention arbitrageur. He understands how the media works, is willing to pay large premiums to buy attention, and then converts that attention into price support for crypto assets.

### Pattern 3: The "acquire + issue token" loop
BitTorrent → BTT | Poloniex → liquidity entry point | HTX → HT/trading volume | Steemit → attempted tokenization (failed). The pattern: acquire a product with a user base → issue a token on top of it → maintain liquidity through exchanges he controls.

### Pattern 4: "Seizing the narrative in a crisis"
The "rescue" operation during the FTX collapse, pushing USDD after the Terra collapse, the global media barrage when the Buffett lunch was postponed: he is good at seizing the narrative position during industry crises, grabbing headlines with grand promises and delivering only part of it through smaller real actions.

### Pattern 5: Investing in political relationships
Grenadian diplomat (2021) → protective layer against the SEC lawsuit → large investments in Trump crypto projects (2024-2025) → SEC case settlement (2026). This is a clear path of accumulating political capital. Every step carries a clear intent to hedge legal/regulatory risk.

### Pattern 6: A persistent contradiction between decentralization ideals and centralized behavior
He publicly advocates a decentralized internet, but in practice he controls exchanges (HTX, Poloniex), used exchange power to seize control of a community (Steem), suppresses media criticism (the CoinDesk incident), and uses political relationships to influence regulation. This is Justin Sun's most central gap between words and actions.

### Pattern 7: Awareness of media control
- In 2023, sent retraction demands to crypto media (involving reporting on terrorism financing)
- In 2024, demanded that CoinDesk delete its article on the banana purchase
- According to citationneeded.news, Justin Sun also took legal action against Bloomberg to control the narrative
- Sharply at odds with his public advocacy of "decentralization" and "free speech"
