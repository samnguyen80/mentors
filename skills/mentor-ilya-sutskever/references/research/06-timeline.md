| Time | Event | Source |

### Marker of an ideological turning point
By the time of GPT-2, Sutskever had begun publicly voicing concern about AI safety. In an interview he said that if you believe AGI will have extremely strong capabilities, then open-sourcing is not a good idea. This marked his shift from "open research" to "cautious release".

## 5. The OpenAI Board Crisis (November 2023)

| Time | Event | Source |
|------|------|------|
| Weeks before November 2023 | Altman reduced Sutskever's role in the company; internal disagreement over the pace of commercialization intensified | CNN, Fortune |

### Background note
As of November 2023, Sutskever held about $4 billion in vested OpenAI equity. In later testimony he confirmed he still held the equity after leaving and that its value had "increased".

### SSI research direction (as of late 2025)
- Core topic: **scalable oversight** — how to verify that an AI is doing what we want it to do when AI capability exceeds human understanding
- Trains on Google TPUs rather than Nvidia GPUs
- Explicitly says it is not in a rush to release a product, but acknowledges it might release earlier if the superintelligence timeline is longer than expected
- In the Dwarkesh interview Sutskever said: another 100x of scale would make a difference, but would not produce a transformative breakthrough — the key bottleneck is ideas, not compute

## 8. Ideological Turning Points

| Time | Turn | Evidence |
|------|------|------|
| 2019 (GPT-2 release) | From "open research" → "cautious release" | Publicly said that if you believe AGI will be extremely powerful, open-sourcing is a bad idea |
| July 2023 | From "capability first" → "safety first" | Formed the Superalignment team, committed 20% of compute, stated that superintelligence could arrive within ten years |
| October-November 2023 | Safety concern turned into action | Wrote the 52-page memo challenging Altman, pushed the board to remove the CEO |
| May-June 2024 | From "reform within the system" → "independent building" | Left OpenAI, founded SSI, with a single mission: safe superintelligence |
| November 2025 | From "scalingism" → "researchism" | Publicly announced the end of the scaling era, the key bottleneck being scientific ideas rather than compute |
