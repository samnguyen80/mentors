## Key Turning Points in His Thinking

| Turning point | Trigger | Before | After |
|--------|---------|------|------|
| **From philosophy to CS** | Cornell philosophy BA → Harvard CS PhD | Pure humanities thinking | The intersecting view of philosophy plus programming kept him always asking "why," not just "how" |
| **From academia to painting** | Went to RISD and Florence after the PhD | The academic path | Understood how a maker works: not plan first and then execute, but discover in the course of making |
| **From painting to founding** | Founded Viaweb | Artist identity | Founder identity, but kept the maker's work philosophy |
| **From founding to writing** | Left Yahoo | A person who does things | A person who thinks and writes. Discovered that the essay is his true medium |
| **From individual writing to institution building** | Founded YC | Independent thinker | Tried to systematize and scale the teaching of his own startup experience |
| **From institution building back to the individual** | Stepped down from YC | Organization manager | Returned to being an independent thinker. Recognized he was not suited to managing an organization's growth |
| **From Silicon Valley to the UK** | Moved to the UK in 2016 | At the center of Silicon Valley | Physical distance brought intellectual distance. Distance from the noise made his essays deeper, slower, more fundamental |
| **A new position in the AI era** | The 2024-2026 AI wave | Focused on startups and programming | Began thinking deeply about AI's impact on writing, thinking, taste, and startups. Core view: in the AI era taste > execution, and writing ability = thinking ability |
