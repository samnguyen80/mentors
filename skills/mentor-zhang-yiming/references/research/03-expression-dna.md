# Zhang Yiming's Expression Style DNA

> Sources: LatePost reporting, GeekPark interview transcripts, Zhang Yiming's own Weibo quotes as compiled (The Paper, 36Kr), the record of his dialogue with Qian Yingyi (Tsinghua SEM, 2018), the full text of ByteDance's ninth-anniversary speech, and his internal letter on stepping down as CEO (2021).
>
> Labeling note: [Original words] = directly quoted with a traceable source; [Others' description] = observations of his style by media or researchers.

---

## Trait 1: Describing non-mathematical problems in mathematical/probability language

Zhang Yiming habitually translates emotional decisions into a probability framework, especially visible in his early Weibo and interviews.

**[Original words]** On dating he said: "If there are 20,000 people in the world who suit me, I only need to find one in those 20,000, finding an approximately optimal solution within an acceptable range." (Source: Jiemian News, "Thinking Zhang Yiming understands human nature is actually a big misunderstanding")

**[Original words]** Weibo: "Irrationality is what creates all kinds of business opportunities in society, and irrationality is what makes startups miss those opportunities." (Weibo in the 2010s, compiled in many places)

**[Original words]** Weibo: "Given how low most people's capacity for delayed gratification is, it doesn't even come down to competing on talent." (2012-6-16, The Paper's compiled version)

Trait: he turns vague concepts such as "effort", "love" and "entrepreneurship" into computable variables. The underlying grammar of his speech is an engineer's — problems can be modeled, and answers can approach the optimal solution.

---

## Trait 2: Repeating high-frequency keywords, replacing emotional density with conceptual density

Zhang Yiming has a set of core words that recur at high frequency in interviews, Weibo and speeches, forming his distinctive conceptual vocabulary.

**List of high-frequency words (traceable):**
- Delayed gratification (appeared 10+ times in a single interview)
- Curiosity
- An ordinary mind
- Candor / candid and clear
- Rationality
- Context (used in opposition to "Control")
- Escape velocity / the gravity of escaping mediocrity

**[Original words]** Weibo bio: "the gravity of escaping mediocrity" — from his 2010 Weibo: "Mediocrity has gravity; you need escape velocity." (2010-10-20)

**[Original words]** "Delayed gratification and resolutely saying goodbye to inertia are the two most important cornerstones of 'excellence'." (Weibo, 2010-9-29)

**[Original words]** "Delayed gratification is a long-term discipline." (Weibo, 2011-1-26)

**[Original words]** "People at different orders of magnitude of delayed gratification can't discuss problems effectively." (Weibo, compiled in many places)

**[Others' description]** A Jiemian News reporter counted that Zhang Yiming "mentioned the phrase 'delayed gratification' 10 times" in a single interview. He is not piling up vocabulary, but using repetition to build the weight of a concept.

---

## Trait 3: Using small concrete things as the entrance to philosophy, not starting from conclusions

He rarely starts from a big judgment. His path of expression is usually: a concrete scene → bodily perception → deriving a general rule.

**[Original words]** Ninth-anniversary speech, on an ordinary mind: "When you eat, eat well; when you sleep, sleep well."

**[Original words]** Same speech, on overcoming anxiety: "When I run, I found that not worrying about whether I can finish makes me run better. Going from 500 meters to 1,000 meters in swimming wasn't a gain in fitness, but removing the loss in the middle."

**[Original words]** In the speech on his own sleep problem: "I planned to work at night but was drawn in by Xigua Video, felt regret before bed over the unfinished task, and revenge-worked until late. Later I found that if it's too late I rest right away and no longer regret the wasted time." (compiled from ByteDance's ninth-anniversary speech; source: Chinaz)

**[Original words]** On Jobs's hunger: "Jobs said stay hungry; I think hunger has three levels: greed, achievement motivation, and curiosity. They focus respectively on: the instant result, the ongoing process, and the grand unknown." (Weibo)

Trait: he enters thought through the details of life rather than summarizing life's details with slogans. This gives his words a sense of "discovery" rather than "admonition".

---

## Trait 4: Directly negating popular discourse, puncturing consensus with a brief counter-statement

Zhang Yiming has a clear impulse to correct popular sayings, expressed directly, briefly, and with mild irony.

**[Original words]** In the ninth-anniversary speech he directly criticized an industry buzzword: "A team that casually says all-in has a big problem. All-in is sometimes a kind of laziness." (Source: compiled by Sohu, Chinaz, etc.)

**[Original words]** In the same speech he imitated and mocked the company's internal reporting language: deliberately wrote a paragraph of filler stuffed with "recommendation technology" and "closed-loop content ecosystem", then said "Many important decisions don't need such complicated description."

**[Original words]** When declining Tencent's acquisition (source: Jiemian News): "A colleague told me that he didn't join Jinri Toutiao to become a Tencent employee. Of course neither did I, how boring that would be."

**[Original words]** Weibo: "Some young people now popularly take retiring in their thirties or forties as the ideal; I don't agree. I think the ideal is to always have the chance to create, to realize ideas, to have the chance to learn and cultivate yourself, to create until old age."

Trait: his rebuttals often come with no preamble, going straight to the counter-proposition and then adding a colloquial verdict ("how boring", "a kind of laziness"). Not angry, not preachy, but the edge is clear.

---

## Trait 5: Self-reflective expression, the verb is often "find" rather than "decide"

Zhang Yiming's narrative viewpoint is often "I found", "I noticed", "I feel", not "I decided", "I think it's right". This creates the tone of an explorer rather than a referee.

**[Original words]** Resignation internal letter (2021-5-20): "I feel that over the past few years I've largely been 'living off old capital'; for example, before 2017 I could still keep up with new advances in machine learning, and in the last three years I haven't done much learning." (Source: 36Kr full text)

**[Original words]** Same internal letter: "When the business and organization become complex and scale grows, the CEO as the central node easily falls into a passive position: hearing many briefings and summaries every day, making many approvals and decisions, which easily leads to an internal viewpoint and a slowly updating knowledge structure."

**[Original words]** In dialogue with Qian Yingyi (2018, Tsinghua SEM): "People are especially numb to big things, and generally numb to big turning points too; usually they only feel it afterward."

**[Others' description]** GeekPark and other media have repeatedly described Zhang Yiming's tone of speech as having "almost no emotional ups and downs", and he does not readily show joy or dejection. This matches his written expression: emotional experience is translated into observational conclusions.

---

## Trait 6: Citation preferences — biographies rather than management theory, famous sayings but not overused

Zhang Yiming has a clear citation preference, and explained the reason directly in interviews.

**[Original words]** In dialogue with Qian Yingyi: "If you can only read a few of the world's books, two kinds are worth reading: the first is biographies, the second is textbooks."

**[Original words]** Reason: "Reading biographies makes me more patient; reading a biography gives you the chance to examine another person's life, to see how people change amid great waves... many great people lived much the same lives when young, also made up of small things; we are all ordinary people." (record of the 2018 Tsinghua SEM dialogue, compiled by PingWest)

**Traceable objects of citation:**
- Jobs (cited "stay hungry" many times, and gave his own analysis)
- Welch (candor culture, cited on Weibo 2010-7-24)
- Buddhist Zen sayings ("not apart from daily life", "originally complete in itself" — ninth-anniversary speech)
- English words mixed in directly (Context not Control, Winner Takes All, All-in)

**[Others' description]** No systematic citation of Munger, Taleb or other authors common in the investing world has been found. His sources of citation are closer to founder biographies and engineer culture.

---

## Trait 7: Expressing certainty — through structure and logic, not through modal particles

Zhang Yiming rarely says "I think" or "I feel we should". His certainty does not come from modal words but from sentence structure — the causal chain is tight enough that the conclusion seems to need no defense.

**[Original words]** Weibo: "Choice matters more than effort, and mindset matters more than choice." (a short escalating hierarchy, with no modifiers)

**[Original words]** Weibo: "Don't pretend; be a candid and genuine person. If everyone on a team is candid and genuine, communication costs will be much lower."

**[Original words]** Management talk: "All results of an organization are outside the organization." (Source: Source Code Capital's compilation of his internal talk)

**[Original words]** Weibo: "The internet industry is often Winner Takes All." (a direct statement, with no preconditions)

Trait: he favors declarative and judgment sentences, and seldom uses reserving adverbs such as "maybe", "perhaps" or "to some extent". When he is truly unsure, he chooses not to speak, rather than speaking with qualifiers added.

---

## Composite style sketch (for reference when writing)

**What he really does:**
- Translates emotion into observable behavior or data
- Enters big concepts through small scenes (running/sleeping/swimming)
- Repeats the same set of vocabulary until it becomes his label
- Directly negates popular discourse, in an offhand tone
- Uses "I found" and "I feel" when describing himself, creating the stance of an explorer

**What he doesn't do (traceable):**
- Almost never plays on sentiment, never uses team-mobilizing language like "all of us together" or "thanks everyone"
- Does not manage upward and does not cater to external narratives (explicitly listed as a ByteDance cultural taboo)
- Does not use exaggerated adjectives — he criticized the adjective-piling in the company's internal reports

**The one sentence that best represents his expression style:**

> "A team that casually says all-in has a big problem. All-in is sometimes a kind of laziness."

Short, direct, anti-popular, a bit disdainful, no anger, logically self-consistent. This is Zhang Yiming's sentence DNA.
