## 1. Dan Koe: The One-Person Business and Personal Brand Framework

### 1.1 Core idea: you are the business

Dan Koe's central claim is "turn yourself into a business." Don't pick a market and then serve it. Productize your own goals, problems, and path to solving them.

**Experience Model**: You are your own customer profile. Solve your own problem, attract people like you, and systematize the solution: "Climb the stairs, then send the elevator back down for the people behind you."

> Source (primary): [The One-Person Business Model - Dan Koe](https://thedankoe.com/letters/the-one-person-business-model-how-to-monetize-yourself/)

### 1.2 The four-pillar framework

| Pillar | Definition | Key actions |
|--------|------------|-------------|
| **Brand** | Your story: what goal you are pursuing and why | Define a vision and an anti-vision (the lifestyle you stand against) |
| **Content** | Your map: guiding the audience from point A to point B | 1-3 months of consistent content to build brand awareness |
| **Product** | Your solution: start with an MVO (minimum viable offer) | Sell first, refine later; start with $500-1000 freelancing or consulting |
| **Marketing** | Your promotion: keep exposing your product | No promotion, no income; for beginners, content itself carries a growth effect |

> Source (primary): [The One-Person Business Model - Dan Koe](https://thedankoe.com/letters/the-one-person-business-model-how-to-monetize-yourself/)

### 1.3 The "Niche of One" positioning method: don't find a niche, create one

Dan Koe is openly against traditional niche selection: "You don't need a niche, you need a point of view."

**Three elements make up your worldview/niche:**

1. **Goals**: goals you set for yourself, not the ones society hands you by default (like retiring at 60)
2. **Problems**: the pain points that stop you from reaching those goals
3. **Potential Paths**: a clear, step-by-step system

**The Interest Stack method:**
- Don't position in a single field. Combine several interests (fitness + philosophy + business + lifestyle)
- Where interests intersect you get a distinctive perspective, which protects you from homogeneous competition
- A narrow niche adds about 5,000 followers a year; a wide niche with a unique combination can add 20,000 a year

**Core formula: "Wide brand, narrow product"**
- At the content level: share the philosophy, education, and practice of your varied interests (without directly selling)
- At the product level: build a static landing page for a specific problem; the product is narrower than the content

> "You're not targeting a niche. You are a niche, and your job is to persuade people to join it."

> Source (primary): [How To Create Your Niche Of One - Dan Koe](https://thedankoe.com/letters/how-to-create-your-niche-of-one-become-nicheless/)
> Source (primary): [You don't need a niche, you need a point of view - Dan Koe](https://thedankoe.medium.com/you-dont-need-a-niche-you-need-a-point-of-view-0a22b5e2802b)

### 1.4 The "Book of Your Life" brand structure

Think of a brand as a book with four chapters:

| Chapter | Content | Suggested share |
|---------|---------|-----------------|
| **Introduction** | Your origin story and transformation narrative | — |
| **Philosophy** | Your answer to "the good life"; defines the enemy and the extreme beliefs | 40% |
| **Education** | Teaching the skills and knowledge within your brand's scope | 40% |
| **Practice** | Creating step-by-step systems and named frameworks (like "The 2 Hour Writer") | 20% |

> Source (secondary, a distillation of Dan Koe's methodology): [A distilled knowledge base for building a personal brand - Dan Koe method](https://digital-garden.ontheagilepath.net/a-distilled-knowledge-base-for-building-a-personal-brand-base-on-dan-koes-method)

### 1.5 The content creation engine: the 2 Hour Writer system

**Core process: a 1-hour walk to find inspiration + 1 hour of writing and editing**

**The 6-step creative thinking process:**
1. Pick one idea from a book, podcast, or social media
2. Ask 3 or more questions about that idea ("Why?" "How?")
3. Write the first draft without judging it, using the PAS framework (Problem-Amplify-Solution)
4. Switch to the reader's view and review it, like someone scrolling their phone who needs to be entertained
5. Anticipate objections and leave room for debate
6. Format: add line breaks, polish the hook, cut the filler

**Idea Museum**:
- Keep an organized library of source material; prefer old or obscure books, curated blogs, and high-quality accounts as sources
- Pick topics at the intersection of "Performance (it could blow up)" and "Excitement (you're interested in it)"

**Quick reference of writing frameworks:**
- **Listicle**: Hook > list of points > conclusion
- **Short post (1-2 sentences)**: a personal redefinition / a "painful truth" / "if this, then that"
- **P-S-B story arc**: Pain point → Solution → Benefit

> Source (primary): [The 2 Hour Writer](https://2hourwriter.com/)
> Source (secondary): [Dan Koe's 2-Hour Writer summary](https://ilyaas.substack.com/p/dan-koes-2-hour-writer)

### 1.6 The content repurposing flywheel

**Core principle: everything starts from the newsletter. Spend 30 minutes a day on the newsletter, and 10 minutes on the rest of the content.**

**The specific process:**

```
Newsletter (long form, 1-2 per week)
    ├── Extract 5-7 short posts → Twitter/X
    ├── Screenshots of Twitter posts → Instagram / LinkedIn
    ├── Read the newsletter aloud → YouTube video
    ├── YouTube audio → podcast platforms
    └── High-engagement tweets → expanded into topics for the next newsletter
```

**Schedule:**
- Sunday: newsletter outline (30-60 minutes)
- Monday-Friday: each day write one section of the newsletter (30-60 minutes) + schedule the daily posts (30 minutes) + engagement and promotion (30 minutes)
- Saturday: publish the newsletter + write promotional posts

**Key strategy: test on Twitter first.** The character limit forces you to distill the core. Once an idea works on Twitter, expand it into a newsletter, and let the newsletter become a YouTube script.

> Source (primary): [Dan Koe Twitter](https://x.com/thedankoe)
> Source (secondary): [Dan Koe's Newsletter Repurposing Content System](https://mikeromaine.com/dan-koes-newsletter-repurposing-content-system/)

### 1.7 Specific Twitter growth tactics

**On the writing side:**
- The hook is the heart of everything; the time you spend on the hook should equal the time you spend writing the rest
- Followers don't want "more content," they want solutions to their problems
- A "sharp opinion" makes you stand out from the sea of sameness

**On the engagement and growth side:**
- **The DJ method**: create a thread that rounds up other people's best posts (for example "Most people are terrible at XX... here are the 7 best posts on it, 5 minutes and you're done"), and tag the original authors. They will repost it and you gain their followers
- **Tribe infiltration**: every day reply to content from your target accounts → move the conversation from replies to DMs → ask how they're growing → propose supporting each other's engagement
- **High-quality replies**: don't write "great post," write a 200-400 word "mini newsletter-style reply"
- **Long-form builds trust**: short content earns exposure, while long content (newsletters, articles) builds deep trust and authority

**A 7-step, non-begging DM networking approach:**
1. Find people who truly align with your goals
2. Send specific praise (about their work)
3. Ask about their goals/projects
4. Offer value first (share resources, make introductions)
5. Optional: move to a call or a deeper conversation
6. Follow up with relevant resources
7. Make a request only after you've built a solid relationship

> Source (primary): [The Best Way To Grow On Social Media - Dan Koe](https://thedankoe.com/letters/the-best-way-to-grow-on-social-media-from-zero-followers-experience/)
> Source (primary): [How To Actually Grow On Social Media - Dan Koe](https://thedankoe.com/letters/how-to-actually-grow-on-social-media-what-they-dont-tell-you/)

## 2. Alex Hormozi: Front-Loaded Value and the Content Machine

### 2.1 Core philosophy: "Give Away the Secrets, Sell the Implementation"

Hormozi's content philosophy fits in one sentence: **give the secrets away for free, and sell the execution.**

- Only 1% of people will actually do it themselves; 99% are willing to pay someone to do it for them
- Give away high-value content for free → prove you have the solution → build trust and reciprocity → conversion follows naturally
- He began executing this strategy in 2022, spent nothing on ads, and gained over 1 million followers within 6 months

**Follower numbers as of August 2025:**
- YouTube: 3.55M
- Instagram: 4M
- X/Twitter: 918K
- LinkedIn: 714K

> Source (primary): [Alex Hormozi on X - "Giveaway the secrets, sell the implementation"](https://x.com/AlexHormozi/status/1529553072136998914)
> Source (secondary): [Content Repurposing Strategy: 180k to 1.2M Followers - Copyblogger](https://copyblogger.com/content-repurposing/)

### 2.2 The Hook-Retain-Reward framework

This is the three-part structure at the core of Hormozi's content strategy:

**1. HOOK: grab attention instantly**

| Technique type | Example |
|----------------|---------|
| Shock/surprise | Drop a counterintuitive fact or number |
| Provocative question | Trigger curiosity with a question |
| Bold promise | A clear, achievable benefit |

> Example: "Most people waste 80% of their marketing budget, and they don't even know it."

**2. RETAIN: keep delivering value**
- Use stories plus open loops (leave suspense) to hold attention
- Every sentence or frame must deliver value, with zero filler
- Break the rhythm with headings, bullet points, and visual elements
- Keep referring back to the audience's pain points

**3. REWARD: over-deliver**
- Give action steps they can take right away
- Deliver more value than you promised (promise 1 bonus, give 3)
- Provide free resources with zero friction
- Make the audience look forward to the next piece of content

> Source (secondary, a systematic analysis of Hormozi's strategy): [Alex Hormozi's Content Strategy: Hook, Retain, and Reward Explained](https://itsmostly.com/blog/alex-hormozis-content-strategy-hook-retain-and-reward-explained)

### 2.3 The Value Equation: writing an irresistible hook

**Formula: Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood) / (Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice)**

The bigger the numerator and the smaller the denominator, the more irresistible the hook.

| Variable | What it does | How to strengthen it |
|----------|--------------|----------------------|
| **Dream Outcome** | The best result the reader can get | Paint a vivid best-case scenario |
| **Perceived Likelihood** | How strongly the reader believes they can do it | Use "step-by-step," "proven system," "fail-proof" |
| **Time Delay** | How fast results show up | Use short time frames (10 days vs 3 months), but don't be unrealistic |
| **Effort & Sacrifice** | How much effort is required | Use "simple steps," "easy-to-follow," "beginner-friendly" |

**How to apply it: before writing a hook, evaluate the four variables systematically:**
1. Can you amplify the desired result and the perception that it's achievable?
2. Can you shorten the timeline and lower the effort required?

> Source (secondary): [How to Use Alex Hormozi's Value Equation to Write Viral Hooks](https://davidschwertfeger.com/newsletter/alex-hormozis-value-equation-to-write-viral-hooks/)
> Source (primary, book): Alex Hormozi, *$100M Offers*

### 2.4 The MAGIC headline formula

From *$100M Offers*, built for naming products/services but usable for content titles too:

| Letter | Meaning | Explanation |
|--------|---------|-------------|
| **M** | Magnet (the magnetic reason) | Give a reason to pay attention, such as "back-to-school discount" or "limited time" |
| **A** | Avatar (customer profile) | Call out your target audience explicitly; the more specific the better |
| **G** | Goal | What the customer is chasing: pain-free, doubled profit, the first customer |
| **I** | Interval (time interval) | How long until they get there? "Pain-free in 10 days" |
| **C** | Container (container word) | Signals this is a packaged set of things: Challenge, Blueprint, Bootcamp |

**Key guideline: you don't need all 5; 2-3 are enough. The shorter the name the better, and the more specific the better.**

> Source (primary, book): Alex Hormozi, *$100M Offers*
> Source (secondary): [Alex Hormozi's MAGIC Headline Copywriting Formula - Breakthrough Marketing Secrets](https://www.breakthroughmarketingsecrets.com/blog/alex-hormozis-magic-headline-copywriting-formula-from-the-100m-offers-book/)

### 2.5 Hook strategy by audience awareness stage

Hormozi adjusts the hook type to how much the audience already knows:

| Awareness stage | Hook type | Example direction |
|-----------------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Unaware** | Curiosity-driven hook | Make people think "what is this?" |
| **Problem-Aware** | Problem-focused hook | Highlight the pain point and the solution |
| **Solution-Aware** | Solution-comparison hook | Why your solution is better |
| **Product-Aware** | Competitor-comparison hook | A direct comparison with the competition |

> Source (secondary): [How I Write Hooks That Actually Work - Alex Hormozi's Proven Method](https://solopreneurcode.substack.com/p/how-i-write-hooks-that-actually-work)

### 2.6 Twitter as a content testing ground

**Hormozi positions Twitter as the "lowest-cost platform for validating ideas":**

1. Post 5 tweets a day to test different ideas
2. Watch which ideas get the most engagement
3. High-engagement tweets → expand into long-form video content
4. Long video → cut 30+ short videos for distribution to Reels/TikTok/Shorts

**Content style traits:**
- Concise, direct, punchy business advice
- Short parallel sentences, with a longer final sentence as the summary
- Tweets about making money perform best, especially those contrasting the mindset of the rich and the poor
- The best day to post is Tuesday

> Source (primary): [Alex Hormozi X Profile](https://x.com/AlexHormozi)
> Source (secondary): [Who's Alex Hormozi? - Favikon](https://www.favikon.com/blog/whos-alex-hormozi)

### 2.7 The content repurposing machine: from 7 to 80 pieces a week

**Growth numbers (within 6 months):**
- YouTube: 70K → 300K
- Twitter: 10K → 100K
- Instagram: 70K → 330K
- SEO traffic: 0 → 22K/month
- Podcast downloads: 20K → 150K/month

**Structure of the 5-person repurposing team:**

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Twitter Editor | Twitter content optimization and scheduling |
| YouTube Editor | Editing long-form YouTube videos |
| LinkedIn Editor | Adapting content for LinkedIn |
| Podcast Editor | Podcast production and distribution |
| Reels/TikTok Editor | Cutting and optimizing short-video clips |

**Total cost: $40,000/month**

**The four-step workflow:**
1. **Validate**: test ideas cheaply on Twitter
2. **Create**: use video as the primary format (video can be split into audio + text + images, but not the other way around)
3. **Edit & Contextualize**: the team cuts clips per platform and inserts CTAs at the start, middle, and end
4. **Distribute & Monitor**: publish on a fixed schedule with tools like Buffer

> Source (secondary): [Content Repurposing Strategy: 180k to 1.2M Followers - Copyblogger](https://copyblogger.com/content-repurposing/)

## 4. Actionable Frameworks Distilled (for Huasheng's X Strategy)

### Framework 1: The three-step brand positioning method (from Dan Koe)

1. **Define your vision and anti-vision**: What kind of life are you after? What do you stand against?
2. **List your Interest Stack**: AI coding + indie development + content creation + personal growth → the intersection yields a unique perspective
3. **Write "The Book of Your Life"**: origin story + philosophical stance + educational content + practice system

### Framework 2: The content creation flywheel (combining both approaches)

```
Twitter posts (3-5 a day, testing ideas)
    │
    ├── High-engagement tweets → Newsletter / WeChat Official Account long-form
    │                    │
    │                    ├── YouTube video script
    │                    └── Xiaohongshu image-and-text posts
    │
    └── Low-engagement tweets → rewrite from a new angle or drop
```

### Framework 3: Hook-writing checklist (from Hormozi)

Before writing each hook, ask yourself:
- [ ] Is the Dream Outcome clear? Can the reader picture the best-case result?
- [ ] Is it credible enough? Is there data, a case, or a system behind it?
- [ ] Is the timeline specific? The shorter, the more attractive
- [ ] Is the barrier low? Does the reader feel they can do it?

### Framework 4: The authority-building path

| Stage | Strategy | Source |
|-------|----------|--------|
| 0-1K followers | The DJ method (curating others' content) + tribe infiltration (high-quality replies) | Dan Koe |
| 1K-10K followers | Daily tweets + a weekly newsletter + sharp opinions | Dan Koe + Hormozi |
| 10K-100K followers | Content repurposing machine + video expansion + free high-value resources | Hormozi |
| 100K+ followers | Named frameworks (like "2 Hour Writer") + a product ecosystem | Both |
