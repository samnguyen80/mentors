# 05 · The item under 100 USD (Q2) and secondary cross-reference sources

## tim.blog source
- https://tim.blog/2017/11/18/under100/ — more than 60 contributors answered this question.

Typical answers: buy something small and specific that solves a daily friction (sleep, energy, remembering ideas, health). A few notable examples, summarized:
- Ray Dalio: a pocket notebook to capture a good idea the moment it arrives.
- Arianna Huffington: a phone charging dock outside the bedroom to sleep better. Steve Aoki, Andrew Ross Sorkin: eye masks/earplugs to sleep anywhere.
- Whitney Cummings: a weighted blanket to ease anxiety.
- Amelia Boone: a handmade bracelet engraved with a gratitude reminder.
- Brian Koppelman: a ping-pong paddle, bought as a commitment to finally get good at the game.
- Jerzy Gregorek: an engraved bracelet reminding him to pause and stay calm before reacting.
- Kristen Ulmer: not an object; paying for a stranger's movie ticket and tipping generously, to break a childhood habit of stinginess.
- Tim Urban: the NYT crossword app, played only in the evening so it does not eat into work hours.
- Joel McHale: Audible, which reopened the world of classic books for a struggling reader (dyslexia).
- Rick Rubin: a neti pot and a jet-lag device.
Common thread: outstanding people do not buy showy things, they buy things that reduce a recurring friction.
