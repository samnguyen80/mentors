## Trajectory of the Evolution of Core Ideas

### The Software 1.0 → 2.0 → 3.0 trilogy

- **Software 1.0** (traditional programming): humans write explicit rules
- **Software 2.0** (proposed 2017): neural networks learn rules from data, weights are code
- **Software 3.0** (proposed 2025): the LLM is itself the program, and English/natural language is the new programming language

### The "engineering realism" forged at Tesla

- Before Tesla: academic-leaning, focused on elegant model architecture
- After Tesla: a deep intuition for the "march of nines" (the engineering climb from 90% to 99% to 99.9%); extremely sensitive to the complexity of "deploying AI in the real world"
- Signature position: firmly supports the pure-vision approach, regarding LiDAR as "a placebo for engineers"

### Three leaps in the education mission

1. CS231n (2015): classroom teaching, at the scale of 750 students
2. YouTube Zero to Hero (2022): free teaching worldwide, an audience in the millions
3. Eureka Labs (2024): institutionalized AI-native education, the "AI teaching assistant + human teacher" model

### Stability of his view of AGI

- Consistently leans "pragmatic/realist", not following the "AGI in 6 months" narrative
- 2025 Dwarkesh interview: AGI still needs 10-15 years
- Skeptical of the over-narrative around agents: "agents aren't magic, they're just LLMs called in a loop"

## Key Developments of the Past 12 Months (2025 to date)

- **The spread and correction of the vibe coding concept** (the hot topic of all of 2025): the original concept was diluted → he proactively corrected it with "agentic engineering"
- **The Software 3.0 framework** (2025-06): a systematic account of English as a programming language
- **microGPT** (2026-02): a new peak of the minimalist education philosophy
- **The "programmer left behind" tweet** (2025-12): resonated worldwide, showing he is willing to share vulnerability publicly
